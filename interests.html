Interests and Activities

Royal Air Force Air Cadets

I have been a Civilian Instructor with the Royal Air Force Air Cadets since June 2024 and currently serve as the Training Officer of 275 Nantyglo and Blaina Squadron. I help plan and deliver training across a range of subjects, including Principles of Flight, Flying Operations, fieldcraft, STEM, and leadership. I also deliver and oversee weapons training and marksmanship activities across several local squadrons. I particularly enjoy teaching and developing younger cadets, and have gained significant experience explaining complex ideas to people of different ages and abilities.

Hobbies

Outside of mathematics, I spend much of my spare time playing squash and golf. I also enjoy solving and collecting Rubik's cubes, which has been a hobby of mine since I was younger. I enjoy learning new solving techniques and trying to improve my times, and it was one of the things that first sparked my interest in problem solving.
