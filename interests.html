Interests

June 2024 – Present

Royal Air Force Air Cadets

I have volunteered with the Royal Air Force Air Cadets since June 2024 and currently serve as Training Officer at 275 Nantyglo and Blaina Squadron. I plan and deliver training across a range of subjects including Principles of Flight, Flying Operations, fieldcraft, STEM, drill, and leadership. I also support weapons training and marksmanship activities across several local squadrons.

Volunteering with the Air Cadets has given me considerable experience in teaching, leadership, organisation, and communicating technical concepts to young people with different levels of knowledge and ability. I particularly enjoy developing lessons and finding ways to make difficult concepts accessible and engaging.

University

Mathematics and Computing

Outside of formal study, I continue to explore areas of mathematics that interest me, particularly algebraic topology, Lie theory, category theory, and the interaction between pure mathematics and computational methods. I also enjoy programming mathematical ideas in Python and experimenting with computational approaches to mathematical problems.

Sport

Squash, Golf and Boxing

I regularly play squash and golf in my spare time. I enjoy the competitive and problem-solving aspects of both sports, particularly the need to adapt tactics and make decisions throughout a match or round.

I was also heavily involved in Cardiff University's Amateur Boxing Club, serving as Treasurer and Vice Captain before becoming President. As President, I helped organise training and club activities, introduced a weekly women-only training session, and organised a boxing home show that raised over £600 for new equipment.

Bronze – Gold

Duke of Edinburgh's Award

I completed my Gold Duke of Edinburgh's Award, building on the Bronze and Silver awards I completed during secondary school. The award involved developing skills outside of education, undertaking physical challenges, volunteering, and completing an expedition. The experience helped me develop independence, resilience, and perseverance.
