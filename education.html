Master of Science in Mathematics, Cardiff University

Graduated with Distinction, achieving an overall mark of 85%. Developed advanced knowledge across pure and applied mathematics, with particular focus on algebra, topology, analysis, and mathematical physics.

My dissertation, Categories and Classifying Spaces, investigated the relationship between simplicial homology and classifying spaces. I used category-theoretic methods, particularly the nerve construction, to associate a simplicial set to a group viewed as a category, and studied the resulting homological and cohomological properties. The project also included explicit computations of classifying spaces and applications of the Universal Coefficient Theorem.

Relevant modules included Lie Algebras (92%), Operator Theory and C*-Algebras (85%), and Mathematical Biology (85%).
