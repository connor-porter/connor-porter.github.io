About

I am a Masters graduate in Mathematics from Cardiff University, where I wrote my dissertation on the applications of category theory to simplicial homology and cohomology, with a particular focus on studying groups by constructing their classifying spaces. From a research perspective, I am keen to explore topology (particularly topological data analysis), Lie theory, and category theory further. My passion for problem solving began in my early-teen years with collecting Rubik's cubes and playing chess, and naturally led me towards a passion for mathematics. I have always enjoyed working through difficult problems and persevering when things don't work out immediately. This is something I have developed through my extracurricular activities, especially through completing my Gold Duke of Edinburgh's Award and becoming president of Cardiff University's Amateur Boxing Club. I am currently exploring pathways into a PhD in mathematics, with the aim of developing my mathematical interests and teaching capabilities further while working on problems that are either in pure mathematics or at the intersection of pure and computational mathematics.

I completed an MSc in Mathematics at Cardiff University, following a BSc in Mathematics.
