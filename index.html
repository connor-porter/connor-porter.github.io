About

I am a Masters graduate in Mathematics from Cardiff University, where I wrote my dissertation on the applications of category theory to simplicial homology and cohomology, focussing on studying groups by constructing their classifying spaces. From a research perspective, I am keen to explore topology (particularly topological data analysis), Lie theory, and category theory further.

I've always enjoyed solving difficult problems, starting with collecting Rubik's cubes and playing chess and eventually a passion for mathematics in my early-teen years. I love working through difficult problems and persevering when things don't work out immediately. This is something I have developed through my extracurricular activities, especially through completing my Gold Duke of Edinburgh's Award and becoming president of Cardiff University's Amateur Boxing Club.

I am currently looking for opportunities to study a PhD in mathematics, with the aim of developing my mathematical interests and teaching skills further while working on problems that are either in pure mathematics or at the intersection of pure and computational mathematics.
